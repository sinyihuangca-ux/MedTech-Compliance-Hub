### Product Description

The TMS therapy system is a computerized, electromechanical medical device that delivers non-invasive magnetic stimulation to targeted regions of the cerebral cortex.

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) uses brief magnetic pulses to induce electrical currents in targeted areas of the brain.

## 7. Project Lessons Learned

### Scope Management

Changes to the planned deliverable should be documented, reviewed, and communicated to relevant stakeholders.

### Issue Tracking

Each issue should have a clear description, priority, responsible person, corrective action, and closure evidence.

### Risk Management

Risk assessments should be supported by appropriate information rather than assumptions. Outstanding manufacturer responses should remain visible in the tracking system.

### Record Consistency

Testing status, documentation review status, issue status, and project status should be managed as separate fields.

### Data Integrity

Product, test, risk, project, and issue records should use consistent identifiers and valid relationships in the database.
